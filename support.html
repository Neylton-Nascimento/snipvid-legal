Common questions

  • Processing failed? Wait 30 seconds and try again. Some platforms rate-limit our server — try a different public link or upload the file directly.
  • Wrong language? Change "Translate summary to" on the Snip tab before you tap Snip & Summarize.
  • History missing? History is stored on your device only. Reinstalling the app clears it.
